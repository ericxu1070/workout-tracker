Plateline is now Repsilon: new icon, new name, and a launch animation. Updating keeps your log.

### Stretching
- New Stretching tab with a live player: get-ready countdown, a continuous timer, paced reps, and spoken cues.
- Famous routines (Limber 11, Simple 6, Starting to Stretch, Sun salutation A) and 26 new animated moves.
- Build your own routines, or copy and edit a built-in one.

### Workout
- Every tab's main action is in its bottom bar. The rest timer lives there too, so logging a set moves nothing.
- Upcoming shows your next workout and the day it will likely fall on.
- Switch between Lifting, Cardio and Stretching mid-lift.
- Water goal follows your body weight and the day's training.

### History and Exercises
- Calendar with a dot for each kind of training, Monday-first weeks, and month stats.
- Swipe left to delete workouts, cardio, exercises, and weigh-ins.
- Muscle map for the day, week, or month. 3D form viewer with male and female bodies in three builds.
