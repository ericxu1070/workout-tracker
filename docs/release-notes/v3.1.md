Two new features and a polish pass on Loaded. Updating keeps your log.

### Top sets
- Log top sets is a third way into a day, beside Start workout and Open session. Each exercise is one line: the weight and reps of your best set, and how many sets you did.
- The lines start from the plan, so a day that went to plan takes one tap per exercise. You can add any other exercise.
- No clock runs. Finish asks how long the workout took, starting from an estimate based on the sets you entered, and names any exercise you did not log.
- A top set that meets the plan moves the exercise on, as a full workout would.
- History shows these days as one row per exercise. Exercise charts draw them as rings.
- You can now fix any finished workout's time by picking its start or its end.

### Strength percentiles
- Squat, bench press and deadlift get a Percentile view. It shows where your best estimated 1RM of the last 90 days falls among lifters of your gender, body weight and age.
- A new Strength page on the Exercises tab ranks the three lifts as a competition total. It also lists each lift's percentile and who you are compared with.
- Hold and drag along the curve to read any percentile's weight and its multiple of body weight.
- Set your birth year on the Strength page. Until you do, you are compared with lifters aged 24 to 34.
- The comparison group is about 244,000 raw, drug-tested lifters from OpenPowerlifting. The table ships with the app, and nothing is fetched.

### Loaded
- Loaded keeps the theme's flat page. The tab bar and the floating switch are frostier.
- Swiped pages cross-fade, and ripples and color wipes keep running when they overlap.
- A tab's last rows clear the floating tab bar.
- A tap near a muscle on the muscle map picks it.
