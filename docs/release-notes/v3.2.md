Quick log, smoother pill selectors, and a round of fixes. Updating keeps your log.

### Quick log
- A new Quick log button on the Workout tab records exercise done on a whim: pick an exercise, then sets and reps, with an optional weight. Bodyweight moves use your body weight.
- Quick logs show in History, exercise charts, estimated 1RM and the muscle map. They never change your program's weights.
- Handstand press is now in the exercise list.

### Pill selectors
- Tapping the tab bar, the Lifting, Cardio and Stretching switch, or any segmented control now slides the pill like a drag: the bar grows, and in Loaded the Liquid Glass runs as it moves.
- The speckled band under the Workout tab's switch should be gone. It never showed on the emulator, so tell us if you still see it.

### Fixes
- Back returns to the screen you came from: from a lift opened on the Strength page, and from an exercise opened inside a workout. A lift opened from Strength starts on its Percentile view.
- Top sets: Add exercise leaves out exercises already in the day, in Open session too. A lift from another program day keeps its tier. Finish names every exercise you have not logged.
- Picking a workout's end time gives whole minutes.
- Strength page: the birth year can be cleared, the age range stays on one line, and the curve draws for very light lifts.
- Food: the custom estimate and its Low and High controls fit on screen, and you can tap a number to type it.
